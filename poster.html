NEXVORA AI

Smart AI-Powered Interview Preparation and Resume Intelligence Platform
⚡ AI Engine
Gen-3
Abstract
NEXVORA AI is a production-oriented SaaS platform engineered to resolve the fragmentation in modern placement preparation. It unifies ATS resume intelligence, skill gap detection, dynamic role-specific coding questions, and voice-assisted company mock interviews into an adaptive learning workflow. Powered by TypeScript, Node.js, and LLM reasoning, NEXVORA provides measurable readiness metrics and targeted skill diagnostics.
Problem Statement
  • Fragmented Prep: Students juggle separate tools for resume building, coding DSA, and mock interviews without unified tracking.
  • Static Question Sets: Generic question banks fail to adapt to individual candidate resumes or specific target tech companies.
  • Opaque ATS Feedback: Job seekers lack actionable diagnostic scores explaining why their resumes fail automated screenings.
  • Absence of Real-time Simulation: Traditional preparation lacks conversational audio-visual mock interview environments.

Core Platform Modules

• Resume Studio & ATS Analyzer: Parses PDF/DOCX candidate resumes, extracts core competencies, detects missing skills, and scores ATS readiness (0–100%).

• AI Interview Lab: Company-tailored simulated interviews (Google, Amazon, Microsoft, Zoho, TCS) with speech synthesis, live audio transcription, and structured scoring.

• Coding Arena: Dynamic LeetCode-style problem generation covering Arrays, Strings, SQL, and System Design with test cases, solutions, and algorithmic feedback.

• Placement Analytics Dashboard: Real-time visualization of placement probability, skill growth curves, weekly streaks, and personalized 3-month career roadmap milestones.
